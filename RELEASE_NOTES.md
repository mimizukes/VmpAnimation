**あさひモス · Asahi Moth** is a four minute surreal anime short about a blonde vampire who sits on vending machines at 2:47 in the morning.

### How to watch

Download **`asahi-moth.html`** below and open it in a desktop browser. Press Play. Headphones recommended.

Space pauses, arrow keys seek, F goes fullscreen, M mutes, S toggles subtitles.

### What's inside

One self contained HTML file. All the animation is drawn live in Canvas 2D and all the sound is synthesised with WebAudio, so there are no video or audio files. The typefaces load from Google Fonts when you're online and fall back to your system Mincho when you're not.

Contains quick cuts and bright full screen colour changes.

*An original story made in homage to the visual style of the Monogatari series. Not affiliated with its creators.*
